Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026. This policy describes SpendWise as reviewed from its HTML app source.

1. Information you enter

You may enter transactions (such as amounts, categories, dates and notes), budgets and app preferences. SpendWise does not require you to create an account in the app source we reviewed.

2. Local storage and how it works

SpendWise saves your information using your browser's localStorage, on your own device. The reviewed app source does not show code that sends stored transaction data to a remote server.

3. How information is used

Your information is used within the app to show your transactions, budgets and summaries, and to remember your preferences.

4. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your information. Because the reviewed source stores data locally, we do not receive your entries through the app source.

5. Exporting and importing

You can export your data as JSON or CSV and import a JSON file. Exported files are saved on your device and are not protected by SpendWise. Keep them somewhere safe and only share them with people you trust.

6. Deleting locally stored information

Use the app's clear-data option, or clear your browser or app data in your device settings. Deletion is permanent unless you have an exported backup. We cannot recover data for you.

7. Security limitations of local storage

Local storage is not a vault. Data is not described here as encrypted. Anyone with access to your unlocked device, browser profile or exported files may be able to see it, and browser tools or other software on your device may be able to read it. Use a device lock and keep your device updated.

8. Third-party services and the separately packaged APK

The Android APK is packaged separately from the HTML source reviewed for this policy. We make no claims here about its dependencies, network access, ads or trackers. Your browser, device and app store may have their own privacy practices, and links to external sites are governed by those sites' policies.

9. Children's privacy

SpendWise is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Children should use the app with a parent or guardian.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above will change when we do.
