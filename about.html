About SpendWise

SpendWise is a personal expense-tracking app built to help people organize their everyday finances in one simple place.

Purpose

Keeping track of spending is easier when it is quick and clear. SpendWise gives you a straightforward way to record money in and out, plan with budgets and look back at your history.

What it does

  • Records expenses and income
  • Supports budgets
  • Keeps a transaction history and spending summaries
  • Exports data as JSON or CSV and imports JSON
  • Lets you clear your stored data

Your data

The app stores your information in your browser's local storage on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details and limitations.
