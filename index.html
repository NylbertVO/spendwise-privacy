Your data stays with you

SpendWise stores the information you enter in your browser's local storage on your own device. In the app source we reviewed, stored transaction data is not sent to a remote server.

Local storage is not a backup. Clearing browser data can remove your records, so export a copy regularly.

Read the full Privacy Policy

What this means

Your transactions, budgets and preferences live on your device. You can export them, import a JSON backup, or clear everything from within the app.
